# Invert

Invert turns its input upside down: high values become low and low values become high.

## When to Use It
Use Invert when you need the opposite of a mask (the flat areas instead of the slopes, for example), or a terrain's negative, such as basins from peaks.

> [!TIP]
> Many nodes can also invert their own output through the [Modifier Stack](../../../ui/interface/property-editor/modifier-stack.md).
