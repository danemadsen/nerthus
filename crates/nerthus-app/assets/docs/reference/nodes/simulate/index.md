# Simulate

Simulate nodes apply process-based terrain changes such as erosion, snow, water, vegetation, debris, or deposition. Use this family when the terrain should react as if shaped by natural forces rather than only by geometric filters.

Start here when you need to:

- erode or weather an existing terrain
- add snow, ice, debris, rivers, lakes, or sea behavior
- create vegetation or growth-style surface coverage
- use simulation as a grounding pass after terrain generation

Useful starting points:

- [Erosion](erosion.md), [ErosionPro](erosion-pro.md), [EasyErosion](easy-erosion.md), [Wizard](wizard.md), and [Wizard2](wizard-2.md) for erosion workflows
- [Thermal](thermal.md), [Thermal2](thermal-2.md), [Debris](debris.md), [Scree](scree.md), and [Sediments](sediments.md) for breakdown and deposition
- [Lake](lake.md), [Rivers](rivers.md), [Sea](sea.md), and [HydroFix](hydro-fix.md) for water-related terrain behavior
- [Snow](snow.md), [Snowfield](snowfield.md), [Glacier](glacier.md), and [IceFloe](ice-floe.md) for cold-climate effects
- [Trees](trees.md) and [Shrubs](shrubs.md) for vegetation-style simulation

## In this section

- [Anastomosis](anastomosis.md): Anastomosis creates interconnected water flow based downcutting, ranging from small pits to large river channels
- [Crumble](crumble.md): Crumble runs a different kind of simulation that feeds off of existing crevices, erosive information, and other subtle details to collapse the terrain
- [Debris](debris.md): Simulates loose debris across the terrain surface to create broken rocky accumulation
- [Dusting](dusting.md): Dusting is a lightweight snow node which adds a thin layer of snow to your scene with a focus on creating light sprinklings of snow
- [EasyErosion](easy-erosion.md): Provides a simplified erosion workflow for quickly adding natural erosion effects
- [Erosion](erosion.md): Erosion is the cornerstone of all terrains
- [ErosionPro](erosion-pro.md): A powerful erosion algorithm designed to enhance terrain shaping capabilities beyond the traditional erosion
- [Glacier](glacier.md): Glacier creates flowing glaciers on mountain tops
- [Hillify](hillify.md): Softens and reshapes terrain into smoother, hill-like forms
- [HydroFix](hydro-fix.md): HydroFix is a low level utility that can help create unbroken flows
- [IceFloe](ice-floe.md): IceFloe creates flat plates of ice inside a water body
- [Lake](lake.md): Lake simulates rainfall on the terrain and creates physically accurate lakes
- [Lichtenberg](lichtenberg.md): Creates branching, lightning-like erosion or fracture patterns
- [Pond](pond.md): Carves a pond basin with an irregular shore into a terrain
- [Rivers](rivers.md): Rivers can instantly generate complex river networks on any terrain, whether it can sustain rivers or not
- [Scree](scree.md): Creates accumulations of loose rock fragments scattered across slopes and terrain edges
- [Sea](sea.md): Sea is a multi-function water surface generator and coastal erosion tool
- [Sediments](sediments.md): Sediments creates a thick layer of generic sedimentation on top of the terrain, which can be used for sand or snow
- [Shrubs](shrubs.md): Adds low vegetation or shrub-like surface growth across the terrain
- [Snow](snow.md): Snow simulates snow fall, melt, and settling like in nature, and controls how the snow adheres and flows along the terrain
- [Snowfield](snowfield.md): Creates broad settled snow coverage across the terrain surface
- [Thermal](thermal.md): Thermal simulates Thermal Erosion that can create talus and debris
- [Thermal2](thermal-2.md): Thermal2 is an evolution of the previous node. It creates Thermal Erosion to create realistic talus and debris
- [Trees](trees.md): Trees creates superficial forests and vegetation. These forests are part of the heightfield while being "detached" enough for various export scenarios
- [Wizard](wizard.md): The Erosion Wizard is a lightweight wrapper for the Erosion node
- [Wizard2](wizard-2.md): A simplified erosion wrapper that provides curated controls for shaping erosion depth, scale, and strength
