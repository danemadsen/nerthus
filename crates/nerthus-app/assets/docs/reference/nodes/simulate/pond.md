# Pond

Pond carves a pond basin into a terrain: a rounded bowl with a natural, irregular shore, worn by a light erosion pass.

## When to Use It
Use Pond when a terrain needs a small body of water's basin at a chosen spot. Fill it with water using [Lake](lake.md) or [Sea](sea.md) after it.

Its main output is the terrain with the basin carved in; its Water, Depth, Shore and Surface outputs stay empty.

## Properties

### Lake Formation

#### Scale

The pond's size.

#### Depth

How deep the basin is carved.

#### Spread

How far the basin's walls spread out, from steep to gentle.

#### Warp

How irregular the shore is.

#### X

The pond's position across the terrain.

#### Y

The pond's position along the terrain.

#### Seed

Randomization seed for the shore's shape.
