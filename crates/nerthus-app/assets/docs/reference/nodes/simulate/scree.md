# Scree

Creates accumulations of loose rock fragments scattered across slopes and terrain edges.

## When to Use It
Use Scree when you need accumulations of loose rock fragments scattered across slopes and terrain edges.

## Properties

### Stones

#### Scale

Controls the overall size of the scree stones. Higher values produce larger rock fragments, while lower values create finer gravel.

#### Height

Sets the maximum elevation or prominence of the scree stones above the terrain surface.

#### Density

Adjusts how many scree stones are distributed across the surface. Higher values increase the number of stones per area.

#### Spread

Determines how widely the scree stones are scattered from their source, affecting the coverage area and natural dispersion.

#### Edge

Controls the accumulation of scree along terrain edges or boundaries, allowing for more pronounced deposits at the base of slopes or cliffs.

#### Seed

Randomization seed for scree placement and variation. Changing the seed produces different scree patterns.
