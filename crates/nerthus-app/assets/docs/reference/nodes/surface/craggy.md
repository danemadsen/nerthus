# Craggy

Turns a terrain's surface into a craggy, rocky, broken landscape.

## When to Use It
Use Craggy when a surface should become craggy, rocky, and broken.

## Properties

#### Size

Controls the overall scale of the craggy features. Higher values produce larger cracked rock formations on the surface.

#### Depth

Sets the depth or prominence of the cracks and rock features, determining how pronounced the craggy shapes appear.

#### Shape

Defines the overall form and pattern of the cracked rock shapes, affecting their outline and distribution.

#### Seed

Randomization seed for generating unique variations of the craggy rock patterns. Changing the seed produces different results.
