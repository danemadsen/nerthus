# FractalTerraces

FractalTerraces creates terraces or stratification of the terrain. The node is fractal in nature, and terracing is performed in multiple octaves giving you a very high level of detail.

You get detailed control over the edge shape, uniformity, rock hardness, and overall character of the terracing.

## When to Use It
Use FractalTerraces when the landform needs stepped stratification with more natural irregularity than a simple terrace effect.

## Properties

### FractalTerraces

#### Spacing

Sets the space between two terraces. Controls the horizontal distance or interval between each terrace step.

#### Octaves

Specifies the number of terraces to generate, including subtle sub-terraces. Higher values create more layers and finer detail.

#### Intensity

Controls the strength of the terracing process. Higher intensities flatten the terraces, making the steps more pronounced.

#### Shape

Defines the general shape of the terraces. Lower values create steeper terraces that follow the terrain's bulk, while higher values produce smoother terraces that protrude outward.

#### Seed

Randomization seed for the node's process. Changing the seed produces different terrace arrangements.

### Advanced

#### Shapes separation

Controls how distinctly individual terrace shapes are separated from each other, affecting the clarity of terrace boundaries.

#### Macro octaves

Sets the number of large-scale terrace layers, influencing the overall structure and major steps in the terrain.

#### Micro shape

Adjusts the fine detail and small-scale shaping of terraces, adding subtle variations to each step.

#### Character

Modifies the overall character or style of the terraces, such as their ruggedness or smoothness.

#### Thickness uniformity

Controls how consistent the thickness of each terrace step is across the terrain. Higher values produce more uniform terraces.

#### Hardness uniformity

Adjusts the consistency of terrace edge hardness, affecting whether terrace boundaries are sharp or soft throughout.

#### Strata details

Adds additional detail to the strata or layers within the terraces, enhancing geological realism.

#### Protect Range

Defines a range within which the original terrain is protected from terracing, preserving natural features in specified areas.

### Tilt

#### Apply Tilt

Enables or disables the tilt effect, allowing terraces to be slanted in a specified direction.

#### Tilt Amount

Sets the degree of tilt applied to the terraces, controlling how steeply they are angled.

#### Direction

Specifies the direction in which the tilt is applied, determining the orientation of the slant.

#### Tilt Seed

Randomization seed for the tilt effect, producing varied tilt patterns when changed.

#### Warp Amount

Controls the strength of the warp effect applied to the terraces, introducing distortion and organic variation.

#### Warp Size

Sets the scale of the warp effect, determining how broad or fine the distortion is.

#### Warp Style

Specifies the style of warping applied to the terraces, affecting the nature of the distortion.
