# GroundTexture

GroundTexture adds superficial detail to the terrain to increase realism.

## When to Use It
Use GroundTexture when a terrain needs fine ground detail to look more real up close.

## Properties

#### Method

Specifies the style of ground texture to generate, affecting the overall appearance and character of the surface.

- **Harsh**: Creates a ground texture with strong, pronounced features and high contrast, simulating rugged or eroded terrain.
- **Rocky**: Generates a texture with rocky details, emphasizing stone-like patterns and roughness typical of stony ground.
- **Rough**: Produces a generally uneven and coarse texture, adding subtle irregularities for a natural, weathered look.

#### Strength

Controls the intensity of the ground texture effect. Higher values make the texture more prominent and detailed.

#### Coverage

Adjusts how much of the terrain surface is affected by the ground texture, determining the extent of the effect.

#### Density

Sets the concentration of texture features across the surface. Higher density results in more frequent and closely packed details.
