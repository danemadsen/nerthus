# Surface

Surface nodes add terrain character, breakup, stratification, and material-like detail. Use this family after the main landform exists and you need the surface to feel rocky, sandy, layered, cracked, weathered, or more visually complex.

Start here when you need to:

- add rock, sand, stones, terraces, or surface breakup
- make a clean terrain feel less procedural
- build stratified or fractured terrain detail
- create surface patterns that respond to the existing terrain shape

Useful starting points:

- [Terraces](terraces.md), [FractalTerraces](fractal-terraces.md), [Stratify](stratify.md), and [Sandstone](sandstone.md) for layered forms
- [Rockscape](rockscape.md), [RockNoise](rock-noise.md), [Outcrops](outcrops.md), [Stones](stones.md), and [Roughen](roughen.md) for rocky character
- [Sand](sand.md), [Distress](distress.md), [Craggy](craggy.md), [Shatter](shatter.md), and [Shear](shear.md) for breakup and erosion-like detail
- [Bomber](bomber.md), [GroundTexture](ground-texture.md), [Pockmarks](pockmarks.md), [Steps](steps.md), and [Grid](grid.md) for specialized surface structure

## In this section

- [Bomber](bomber.md): The Bomber node takes an input heightfield and "stamps" or bombs it across the entire surface of the terrain using the randomization properties you set
- [Bulbous](bulbous.md): Bulbous inflates existing shapes to make them more voluminous while respecting standard geological boundaries
- [Contours](contours.md): Creates map-like contours for a terrain. Useful for making maps or for other overlays
- [Craggy](craggy.md): Turns a terrain's surface into a craggy, rocky, broken landscape
- [Distress](distress.md): Adds superficial wear, breakup, and irregular damage to a surface
- [FractalTerraces](fractal-terraces.md): FractalTerraces creates terraces or stratification of the terrain
- [Grid](grid.md): Generates a regular grid pattern useful for cartography, masking, or stylized surfaces
- [GroundTexture](ground-texture.md): GroundTexture adds superficial detail to the terrain to increase realism
- [Outcrops](outcrops.md): Outcrops creates rocky outcrops on any surface. You can control coverage, breakage, and surface details. Several Outcrops nodes can be layered for complex results
- [Pockmarks](pockmarks.md): Creates scattered pits, dents, and small impact-like depressions on the surface
- [RockNoise](rock-noise.md): RockNoise is a useful tool for generating fields of rocks with size variations and controllable density
- [Rockscape](rockscape.md): Creates broad rocky surface formations and broken stone-like terrain detail
- [Roughen](roughen.md): Adds fine roughness and small-scale breakup to the terrain surface
- [Sand](sand.md): Sand creates medium and small level sand patterns. It can also be used to simulate dunes
- [Sandstone](sandstone.md): Creates layered sandstone-like terracing and sedimentary rock structure
- [SBomber](s-bomber.md): Scatters copies of other shapes over a terrain, on a grid that tiles seamlessly
- [Shatter](shatter.md): Shatter transforms simple shapes into complex, highly eroded, and fractured terrains
- [Shear](shear.md): Shear creates rock shearing and exposes strata
- [Steps](steps.md): Creates stepped formations and hard terrace-like breaks in the terrain
- [Stones](stones.md): Scatters stones or small rock forms across the surface
- [Stratify](stratify.md): Stratify creates broken strata or rock layers on the terrain in a non-linear fashion, producing a robust, realistic formation
- [Terraces](terraces.md): Creates stepped terrace formations from the terrain
