# Rockscape

Creates broad rocky surface formations and broken stone-like terrain detail.

## When to Use It
Use Rockscape when the terrain needs broad rocky massing or broken stone forms layered onto the existing landform.

## Properties

#### Size

Controls the overall scale of the rock formations. Higher values produce larger, more expansive rock features.

#### Erosion

Adjusts the amount of erosion applied to the rockscape, simulating natural weathering and smoothing of rock surfaces.

#### Breakage

Determines the degree of fracturing and splitting in the rocks, creating more jagged and broken surfaces as the value increases.

#### Roughness

Sets the surface roughness of the rocks, adding fine detail and texture to the formations.

#### Jitter

Introduces randomness to the placement and shape of rock features, making the rockscape appear more natural and less uniform.

#### Clusters

Controls the clustering behavior of rock formations, affecting how rocks group together or remain isolated.

- **Off**: Disables clustering, resulting in evenly distributed rock features.
- **Large**: Creates large clusters of rocks, forming prominent groupings and massifs.
- **Small**: Generates small, tight clusters of rocks, producing scattered groupings across the surface.
- **Shattered**: Simulates shattered rock formations, with highly fragmented and irregular clusters.

#### Relief

Specifies the style of surface relief, affecting the sharpness and layering of the rockscape.

- **Off**: No additional relief is applied; the surface remains as generated by the base parameters.
- **Sharp**: Applies sharp, pronounced relief, emphasizing angular features and steep transitions.
- **Soft**: Applies soft, gentle relief, smoothing out transitions and creating a more weathered appearance.
- **Stratified**: Adds visible stratification or layering to the rockscape, simulating sedimentary or banded rock formations.

#### Seed

Randomization seed for generating unique variations of the rockscape. Changing the seed produces different rock patterns.
