# Plates

Plates is a new primitive, based on a Perlin core with flat plate-like formations created in between larger *Perlin-esque* shapes. The flat, slanting areas can make great slopes, especially when combined with Displace nodes.

## When to Use It
Use Plates when you want a landform base with broad shapes separated by flatter plate-like regions that can be eroded into mesas, basins, or fractured uplands.

## Properties

#### Scale

The scale of the overall field of shapes. Controls the size of the tectonic plates or regions generated by the node.

#### Range

Adjusts the variation in elevation or intensity between different plates, affecting the contrast and diversity of the generated regions.

#### Falloff

Controls the smoothness of the transitions between plates. Higher values create softer, more gradual boundaries; lower values produce sharper edges.

#### Warp

Applies distortion to the plate boundaries, introducing irregularity and natural variation to the shapes.

#### Angle

Sets the orientation or rotation of the plate pattern, allowing you to control the directional flow of the generated regions.

#### Seed

The randomization pattern or seed for the node's process. Changing the seed produces different plate arrangements while keeping other settings the same.
