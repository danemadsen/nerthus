# Volcano

Generates a volcanic landform with a central cone and crater structure.

## When to Use It
Use Volcano when you need a self-contained volcanic cone or caldera that can later be eroded or merged into a broader terrain.

## Properties

#### Scale

Controls the overall size of the volcano feature in the terrain. Increasing the scale makes the volcano larger relative to the surrounding landscape.

#### Height

Sets the maximum elevation of the volcano. Higher values produce taller volcanic structures.

#### Mouth

Defines the diameter of the volcano's crater opening. Adjusting this parameter changes how wide or narrow the mouth appears.

#### Bulk

Determines the mass and thickness of the volcano's body. Higher bulk values result in a more massive and robust volcano shape.

#### Surface

Specifies the surface characteristics of the volcano, allowing selection between different terrain styles for the outer slopes.

- **Smooth**: Applies a smooth surface to the volcano, minimizing roughness and erosion effects for a clean, uniform appearance.
- **Eroded**: Applies erosion effects to the volcano's surface, simulating weathered and worn terrain with more natural detail.

#### X

Sets the horizontal position of the volcano on the terrain grid. Adjust to move the volcano left or right.

#### Y

Sets the vertical position of the volcano on the terrain grid. Adjust to move the volcano up or down.

#### Seed

Defines the randomization seed for procedural generation. Changing the seed produces different volcano shapes and features.
