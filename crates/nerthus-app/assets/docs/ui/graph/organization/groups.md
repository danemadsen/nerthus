# Groups

A group is a framed area of the graph that holds nodes and notes. Groups name parts of a graph ("Base Shape", "Erosion", "Color"), move them together, and can be folded away when you are not working on them.

## Creating a Group

Select the nodes and press **Ctrl + G**. The group is drawn around them. Nodes and notes dropped into a group's frame join it; dragged out, they leave it.

## Working with Groups

- Drag a group by its header to move it with everything in it, and by the corners at its bottom to resize it.
- Double-click the header to collapse the group into a small square, and again to open it.
- The menu button on the header offers:
  - **Group to Tab** – moves the group's nodes to a graph tab of their own.
  - **Rename** (**F2**).
  - **Lock** (**Ctrl + L**) – keeps the group and its nodes from being moved.
  - **Color** and **Icon** – the group's tint and the icon beside its name.

Groups are listed in the Data View's [Terrain View](../../interface/data-editor/terrain-view.md), with their nodes inside them.
