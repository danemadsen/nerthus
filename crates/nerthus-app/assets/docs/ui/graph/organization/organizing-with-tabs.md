# Organizing with Tabs

A project can hold several graphs, each on a tab along the top of the graph. Tabs split large projects into parts (the base terrain on one, the texturing on another) while they still work as one graph: nodes on different tabs connect through [portals](portals-and-chokepoints.md).

## Working with Tabs

- Right-click the tab strip and choose **New Tab**, or use Graph › **New Graph Tab**, to add a tab.
- Click a tab to show its graph; double-click it to rename it.
- Right-click a tab for:
  - **Tab to group** – moves the tab's nodes into a group on another tab.
  - **Rename**.
  - **Delete** – removes the tab and its nodes (a project keeps at least one tab).
  - **Color** – the tab's tint.

A group can go the other way with **Group to Tab** in its menu (see [Groups](groups.md)). Tabs, with their groups and nodes, are listed in the Data View's [Terrain View](../../interface/data-editor/terrain-view.md).

The project opens on the tab it was saved showing.
