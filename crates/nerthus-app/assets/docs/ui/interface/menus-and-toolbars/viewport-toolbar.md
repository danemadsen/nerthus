# Viewport Toolbar

The Viewport Toolbar runs down the right side of the viewport. Its top half holds the preview resolution, the lighting, the render style, the viewport's helpers and the screenshot tools; its bottom half the viewport's options, the measurement tools and the 2D viewport.

**Preview Resolution**: The resolution the viewport previews at: 512 x 512, 1024 x 1024, 2048 x 2048 or 4096 x 4096. The button shows the current one.

**Lighting**: Opens the lighting panel: a dial to place the sun, then the **Sun** (azimuth and elevation), **Visualization** (ground brightness and the mask gradient), **Light** (exposure, sun and ambient intensity, shadows and ambient occlusion) and **Atmosphere** (air density, haze, ozone, the skybox and fog) settings. **Reset Sky** restores the defaults.

**Render Style**: How the terrain is drawn: Realistic Render, Clay Render, or Visualize Data (Render Off), which shows the raw data. See [Render Modes](../viewport/render-modes.md).

**Viewport Helpers**:

* **Grid**, **Bounds** and **Navigation Compass**: Show or hide the grid, the terrain's bounds, and the compass.
* **Overlay**: A pattern drawn over the render to judge scale and placement, and its opacity. **Custom** loads an image of your own. See [Visualizing Scale](../../../using/managing-graphs/visualizing-scale.md).

**Screenshot Tools**: **Save Screenshot** (**F9**) saves the viewport to the screenshots folder. The options save the graph or the viewport to a file you choose (**Ctrl + F9** for the viewport), open the screenshots folder, and set the screenshot's resolution (1x, 2x or 4x the viewport's size).

At the bottom:

**Viewport Options**:

* **Embedded** and **Floating**: Keep the viewport in the main window, or move it into a window of its own.
* **Load Camera...** and **Save Camera...**: Restore a saved camera view, or save the current one.
* **Reset to Loaded...**: Returns the camera to the view saved with the project.
* **Reset to Default...**: Returns the camera to the default view.

**Measure**: **Pick Height/Color** and the **Measurement Tools**. See [Measurement Tools](../viewport/measurement-tools.md).

**Toggle Docked 2D Viewport**: Shows or hides the 2D viewport inside the viewport. See [2D Viewport](../viewport/2d-viewport.md).
