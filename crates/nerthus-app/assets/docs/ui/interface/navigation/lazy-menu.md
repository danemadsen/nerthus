# Lazy Menu

The Lazy Menu can be brought up anywhere in the primary Nerthus UI by pressing `F1`. The Lazy Menu gives you instant access to various areas of the UI without having to remember specific shortcuts.

> [!NOTE]
> In true lazy fashion, when you use the Lazy Menu to navigate to a section, your cursor will also be moved there!
>
> This extra level of laziness can be disabled in the [General](../options/general.md) Options.

## Editing Options

You may not want all options to be visible, or you may want some options to stand out over others. You can edit the Lazy Menu in Options › [Lazy Menu](../options/lazy-menu.md).

This modifies the Lazy Menu with your desired levels of visibility.
