# Viewport

The viewport shows the previewed node as a 3D terrain. It sits above the graph, or in a window of its own (Viewport Options › Floating).

- **Previewing**: the viewport shows the selected node, or the node the preview is locked to (**F**, see [Lock Preview](../../graph/basic-workflow/lock-preview.md)). Masks and color maps show over the [underlay](../../graph/basic-workflow/underlays.md) terrain.
- **Navigating**: drag with the left mouse button to rotate, the middle button to pan and the right button to zoom, by default; Options › Navigation changes the buttons and their speed. Click the viewport first and the keyboard moves the camera too: **W A S D** or the arrows move, **Q**/**E** go up and down, **H**/**K** rotate, **U**/**J** tilt, **O**/**L** zoom, and **Space** or **Home** centres the terrain.
- **Resolution**: the preview builds at 512 to 4096 pixels (the toolbar's resolution button); higher shows more detail and takes longer.

The [Viewport Toolbar](../menus-and-toolbars/viewport-toolbar.md) beside it holds the render style, lighting, the 2D viewport, screenshots and the viewport's options.

- [Render Modes](render-modes.md): How the terrain is drawn
- [2D Viewport](2d-viewport.md): The terrain seen from above, docked or in its own window
- [Measurement Tools](measurement-tools.md): Picking heights and colors, and measuring distances
