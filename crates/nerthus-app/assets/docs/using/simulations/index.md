# Simulations

Nerthus's wide range of simulations help you mimic nature through simulating real-world processes from a both scientific and artistic point-of-view.

The most important simulation is [ErosionPro](../../reference/nodes/simulate/erosion-pro.md) as well as the classic [Erosion](../../reference/nodes/simulate/erosion.md). These are augmented by [Thermal2](../../reference/nodes/simulate/thermal-2.md), [Crumble](../../reference/nodes/simulate/crumble.md), [Sediments](../../reference/nodes/simulate/sediments.md), and others.

[Snow](../../reference/nodes/simulate/snow.md), [Snowfield](../../reference/nodes/simulate/snowfield.md), and [Dusting](../../reference/nodes/simulate/dusting.md) create snow simulations. While [IceFloe](../../reference/nodes/simulate/ice-floe.md) can create floating chunks of ice.

[Lake](../../reference/nodes/simulate/lake.md), [Sea](../../reference/nodes/simulate/sea.md), and [Rivers](../../reference/nodes/simulate/rivers.md) create various kinds of water simulations.

[Trees](../../reference/nodes/simulate/trees.md) and [Shrubs](../../reference/nodes/simulate/shrubs.md) powerful ways to create vegetation masks driven by real geological factors which can then be used later in DCC apps to create entire forests.

[Debris](../../reference/nodes/simulate/debris.md) creates thousands (even millions) of physics-enabled debris onto your terrain.
