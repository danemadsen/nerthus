# Workflows

The Using Nerthus section covers the practical workflows that turn a terrain graph into a finished asset. These pages focus on how the major systems work together: shaping the initial landform, applying erosion and simulations, creating masks and textures, managing larger graphs, and preparing the final build.

Start with [Understanding Erosion](understanding-erosion/index.md) and [Crafting the Surface](crafting-the-surface/index.md) when you are building the terrain itself. Move to [Colorizing and Textures](colorizing-and-textures/index.md) once the landform needs masks, data maps, and color outputs. Use [Managing Graphs](managing-graphs/index.md) and [Baking Nodes](baking-nodes/index.md) when the project grows large enough that organization and iteration speed become important.

## In This Section

- [Understanding Erosion](understanding-erosion/index.md): Erosion is a fundamental process in the natural world that plays a crucial role in shaping terrains
- [Simulations](simulations/index.md): Nerthus's wide range of simulations help you mimic nature through simulating real-world processes from a both scientific and artistic point-of-view
- [Crafting the Surface](crafting-the-surface/index.md): Shape and refine terrain forms with primitives, erosion, surface nodes, and transposed detail
- [Colorizing and Textures](colorizing-and-textures/index.md): Create color maps, masks, data maps, and layered textures for terrain look development and export
- [Importing Terrains](importing-terrains/index.md): Bring external heightfields, tiled datasets, color maps, and meshes into Nerthus for enhancement or integration
- [Managing Graphs](managing-graphs/index.md): Organize, inspect, and simplify larger Nerthus graphs with modifiers, portals, accumulators, data views, and scale tools
- [Baking Nodes](baking-nodes/index.md): The Baking feature in Nerthus is a powerful tool designed to enhance workflow efficiency by locking nodes at a high resolution, enabling high-quality
- [Build and Export](build-and-export/index.md): Prepare final terrain builds, export heightfields, meshes, masks, tiles, and software-specific outputs
