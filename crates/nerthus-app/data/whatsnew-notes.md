# Compatibility

Nerthus reads and writes `.terrain` projects in format version 2.3.1.0. Projects from other versions of the format open too, but test a project or two before moving a pipeline over.

***

**Left out on purpose**: links to Houdini, Unity and Unreal (their nodes, plugin installers and bridges), Network Build and Share. Nerthus's satellite palettes are its own rather than Gaea's, so SatelliteMap, SuperColor (and Cartography through a SatelliteMap) and Weathering give somewhat different colours from Gaea's, and TextureBase with New accentuation a somewhat different mask.

**Useful Hint**: Nerthus keeps its settings, logs and autosaves in its data folder (`~/.nerthus`, or `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Nerthus` on Windows). Tools › Artifact Explorer shows how much room they take.
