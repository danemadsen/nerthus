# Compatibility

Nerthus reads and writes `.terrain` projects in format version 2.3.1.0. Projects from other versions of the format open too, but test a project or two before moving a pipeline over.

***

**Left out on purpose**: links to Houdini, Unity and Unreal (their nodes, plugin installers and bridges), Network Build and Share. Nerthus's satellite palettes are its own rather than Gaea's, so colours from a SatelliteMap or SuperColor differ a little from Gaea's.

**Useful Hint**: Nerthus keeps its settings, logs and autosaves in its data folder (`~/.nerthus`, or `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Nerthus` on Windows). Tools › Artifact Explorer shows how much room they take.
